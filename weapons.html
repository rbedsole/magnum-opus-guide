Weapon Locations & Modifications

Where added weapons appear and how their receiver systems work.

How Magnum Opus installs these mods

Magnum Opus is a curated Wabbajack list. During normal installation, players are not asked to choose individual weapon packs or optional files. The list author decides what is bundled and enabled. A Nexus page calling a file “optional” does not mean that Magnum Opus players must select it.

Verified included: The official Magnum Opus version 9.4.0 changelog explicitly lists Uniques of the Wasteland, AKs of the Wasteland, Bolt Actions of the Wasteland, Lever Actions of the Wasteland and Pump Actions of the Wasteland as added. This is evidence for inclusion in the curated list, not a guarantee that every file offered on those Nexus pages was bundled.

Unresolved: Which individual Uniques of the Wasteland plugin files are included in the published profile, and whether every documented unique weapon is obtainable. The author changelog does not enumerate those plugin filenames. Until the curated plugin manifest is verified, location leads remain mod-author documentation rather than confirmed pack-specific spawns.

Source: Magnum Opus changelog, 2026-08-18, version 9.4.0 · Official Wabbajack installation instructions.

Magnum Opus-specific load order findings

The official 9.4.0 changelog confirms Uniques of the Wasteland was added alongside AKs, Bolt Actions, Lever Actions and Pump Actions of the Wasteland. This establishes inclusion at the modlist level, but not which individual optional plugins are active.

That same update deliberately removed overlapping weapon mods (including standalone AK400, AK74M, Mossberg 500 and Remington 870) and removed AKM, SPAS-12 and M9A1 variants from Combined Arms. When searching for those guns, prefer the newer consolidated weapon families rather than older standalone-mod guides.

The 9.4.0 notes also say the list author changed leveled-list and loot configuration, so original mod-author spawn thresholds are reference points, not confirmed Magnum Opus guarantees.

Confirm exact unique plugins in your installation

  1. Launch the Mod Organizer 2 instance that came with Magnum Opus.
  2. In the left mod pane, search for Uniques and verify that the relevant mod is enabled.
  3. In the right Plugins tab, filter for Unique, Wasteland, or names listed on the mod author's files page. Check which ESP/ESL modules are active.
  4. Check the mod's installed version and compare the active modules to its Nexus documentation.
  5. Only then treat a specific unique weapon location as a confirmed destination for that installation.

Evidence: Magnum Opus changelog, version 9.4.0 · Uniques of the Wasteland documentation.

AKs: documented platform variants

The AKs mod combines AK, Galil and Type 93 Chinese Assault Rifle parts. Examples from its documentation include AKS-74U, AK-112, Type 93 LMG, Galil AR and Galil ARM. The precise spawn thresholds are still unverified. AKs documentation.

Uniques of the Wasteland

AKs of the Wasteland: confirmed spawn rules

The original AKs mod description explicitly states that its AK, Galil and Type 93 families appear in Raider and Gunner leveled lists after level 5, with Gunners generally receiving better-equipped examples. The author also documents randomized attachments and separate Receiver (weapon identity) and Internals (damage/requirements) slots. This replaces our earlier “unknown threshold” note.

Optional crafting patch: verified behavior

The Uniques of the Wasteland author offers an optional crafting file that allows crafting non-unique weapons at a chemistry station, requiring YK9's Crafting Keywords. Do not assume Magnum Opus includes this optional file. No specific ingredient costs are confirmed.

Version and installation warning: Uniques of the Wasteland offers ESP and ESL builds, which the author says must not be mixed. Its optional no-injection patch changes how base weapons enter leveled lists. The mod was added in Magnum Opus 9.4.0. The list author selects the bundled files; confirming the curated plugin manifest would establish which unique-weapon modules are included.

Weapon modification: two important slots

ARs of the Wasteland: The mod author describes receivers determining the gun variant, while the separate Internals slot controls damage and requirements. Its barrel rechamber slot can select among 10mm, 5mm, 5.56mm and .308.

Bolt Actions of the Wasteland: The same receiver-versus-Internals concept applies. Its barrel rechamber slot supports .38, 5.56mm, .308 and .50 BMG, according to the mod page.

These are mod-level features, not a guarantee that every receiver or ammunition option is immediately craftable in Magnum Opus. Verify perk and material requirements at the weapon workbench.

How to identify a rifle in the wild

  1. Inspect its receiver or weapon name; the platform can represent multiple gun variants.
  2. Check the Internals slot before assuming a better receiver means higher damage.
  3. Check barrel rechambering before buying or stockpiling ammunition.
  4. Look at the actual installed crafting requirements; Magnum Opus may apply patches.
