Crafting & Equipment Systems

Gameplay guidance with primary sources and clear version caveats.

Power armor repair loop

  1. Locate an Engineering Workbench.
  2. Craft Power Armor Repair Kits when you have the required materials, or buy them from Atom Cats / Brotherhood vendors.
  3. Keep kits available before difficult fights, since Bastion makes individual power armor pieces matter.

Crafting changes that matter

In Magnum Opus 9.5.1, bandages and morphine injectors were removed from the list of craftable items. If an older walkthrough says to make them, that advice is out of date.

Equipment and Crafting Overhaul (ECO)

The Magnum Opus changelog records the addition of Equipment and Crafting Overhaul, along with patches for several armor and faction outfit mods and legendary modification extensions. ECO can affect workbench and equipment customization workflows, but its precise recipes and enabled options depend on Magnum Opus patches and configuration.

Safe workflow for unfamiliar recipes

  1. Identify the workbench and the exact item name in-game.
  2. Look up the originating mod's description and requirements.
  3. Check whether Magnum Opus supplies a crafting patch or recipe change.
  4. Confirm the displayed material cost in your installed version before committing scarce components.
Verification boundary: The changelog confirms ECO's inclusion historically, not a complete current recipe catalog. We have not verified the exact installed ECO file version or all available crafting stations.

Armor and equipment additions

The 9.0.0 changelog added CROSS_BrotherhoodRecon, Tumbajamba's Brotherhood Armor Collection and Minuteman Combat Armor. The 9.5.0 changelog added Inclusive Power Armor. These are documented additions, but their current distribution and crafting requirements are not yet verified.
