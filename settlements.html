Settlements & SS2

What Sim Settlements 2 adds

Magnum Opus includes the Sim Settlements 2 All Chapters Pack, bringing the story and settlement progression of Chapters 1–3 into the modlist.

Recommended early approach

  1. Find and follow the Sim Settlements 2 introduction to learn plot placement.
  2. Develop one manageable settlement first rather than automating every workshop immediately.
  3. Use residential, agricultural, commercial and industrial plots to develop your settlement economy.
  4. Explore city plans and mayor automation once you understand their resource requirements.
  5. Advance the SS2 story for additional settlement systems and faction conflict.

This is a recommended learning order, not a mandatory quest route.

Build limits

Approach the workshop and press Space, then choose Configure Build Limit. The author warns that increasing the limit too far can harm performance.

Important: The Magnum Opus FAQ warns not to enter Workshop Mode with a jet pack equipped, because doing so can permanently break that jet pack's functionality.

Detailed quest and system reference

The official Sim Settlements 2 wiki provides the in-depth documentation for plots, resources, city plans, and chapters. This handbook focuses on how those systems fit Magnum Opus.

Where the SS2 documentation lives

For the actual systems and quest walkthroughs, use the official Sim Settlements 2 wiki. It is the primary source for plot types, city plans, resources, settlers, and story progression. Magnum Opus's own FAQ separately documents its N plot-upgrade shortcut and workshop build-limit menu.

Pack-specific caution: The official SS2 wiki describes the underlying mod. Magnum Opus can include additional settlement addons, patches and configuration. Check the current MO2 list before assuming an optional SS2 feature is enabled.
