Beginner's Guide terminal

Practical advice and warnings from the Magnum Opus terminal outside Vault 111, recorded from 14 player screenshots.

Source distinction: These are paraphrases of the modlist author's in-game guidance, not independently tested mechanics. Check current mod documentation when a specific mechanic needs further detail. The perk entry visibly ends mid-sentence in the game; nothing is inferred from the missing ending.

Combat, loot & healing

  • Legendary drops: Legendary enemies still appear, but not all yield legendary equipment. The terminal identifies humans, synths, super mutants and robots as eligible; insects and other creatures are not.
  • Crippled limbs: Stimpaks do not restore all broken limbs in this setup. The terminal directs players to Med-X for limb recovery.
  • Laser musket: The crank-charged laser musket has infinite ammunition in Magnum Opus, making it a useful early-game fallback when supplies are scarce.
  • Survival: Survival Mode is explicitly unsupported by the author, who warns of bugs and unbalanced damage.

Sanctuary, the Triangle of Death & SS2

Triangle of Death: Sanctuary, Red Rocket and Abernathy Farm are close enough that the game may load and render all three simultaneously. The author recommends developing only one of these heavily and using city plans in no more than one, if any. This is a stability recommendation, not a restriction on building.

Start Sim Settlements 2

  1. Claim a settlement workshop. The terminal recommends somewhere other than Sanctuary.
  2. Build a Recruitment Beacon (Power → Misc) and a Generator.
  3. Connect the beacon and generator with a wire.
  4. Watch for the stranger who approaches to begin the SS2 storyline.

The author's preferred settlement for this is Starlight Drive-In, outside the Triangle of Death. For plot types and quest details, consult the official Sim Settlements 2 wiki.

Workshop Mode: avoidable issues

  • Settlement trash: The author's cleanup mod removes many non-vanilla-scrappable decals and bushes. Scrap a trash can near a workshop to remove the surrounding garbage of that type. The terminal explicitly says this does not automatically scrap tires, mailboxes or trees.
  • Invulnerability bug: If damage and radiation stop affecting you after Workshop Mode, enter Workshop Mode again, wait a few seconds, then exit.
  • Health dropping to zero: Workshop Mode can interact badly with temporary health-changing effects from food, water or chems. Avoid entering while those effects are active. The terminal suggests waiting with T for them to expire.
  • Jetpacks: Remove your jetpack before entering Workshop Mode or workshop flight may stop working. If it malfunctions, the terminal suggests taking it to a workbench and modifying it.
Practical caution: Save before major settlement work and do not assume increasing build limits fixes engine performance constraints.

Crafting, gear unlocks & ammunition

Standalone workbenches: Magnum Opus uses Whisper's Standalone Workbenches so equipment is crafted at specialized stations instead of crowding everything into the Chemistry Station. For example, armor uses an Armorsmith Workbench and weapons use a Weaponsmith Workbench. The terminal says a free collection of these benches can be found in the Abandoned Tunnels near Red Rocket, accessible through the sewers.

Unlocking equipment: Many weapons require perks and levels, with Gun Nut, Blacksmith and Heavy Gunner named as examples. Armor and clothing crafting is frequently quest-gated. The terminal cites Brotherhood of Steel quests for Brotherhood outfits and the Hunter/Hunted quest for Courser gear. Specific recipes should be checked in-game before planning a build.

Ammo economy: Ammunition drops are less common than in vanilla Fallout 4. The terminal recommends building an Ammunition Workbench, breaking down ammunition you do not need, and using the recovered materials to craft the types you use. It calls this a core early-game mechanic. The Scrounger perk can also help.

Perk overhauls: read the actual chart

The default Magnum Opus perk setup combines Creative Perks, Creative Perks Plus and other custom changes. The terminal warns that the Creative Perks mod page does not accurately represent every perk in this list. Livelier Perks is discussed separately as a distinct overhaul; do not apply its spreadsheet to the default perk setup.
