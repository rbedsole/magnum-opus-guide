Character Progression & Perks

Gameplay guidance with primary sources and clear version caveats.

Which perk chart is in Magnum Opus?

The default Magnum Opus profile has its own customized perk chart. The separate paid Livelier Perks profile is optional and is not required. Do not use vanilla Fallout 4 perk rankings or Livelier Perks screenshots as an exact reference for the default profile.

Planning a build without guessing

  1. Open the default profile's in-game perk chart and read the requirements of the next two ranks you want.
  2. Choose a primary combat style, then reserve perk points for its weapon and damage-support perks.
  3. Account for the list's cover penetration and Bastion power armor systems when choosing equipment.
  4. Revisit perk choices as you unlock new weapons and workshop systems.
Not yet verified: The complete customized perk tree, every rank requirement and the exact effects of all custom perks are not published in the sources reviewed here. This chapter deliberately does not invent a perk-by-perk build.

Author's complete perk chart spreadsheet

The Magnum Opus author published a full perk-chart reference spreadsheet with the May 2024 version 7.0.19 changelog. This is a much better starting point than generic Fallout 4 perk lists. Important: It is a historical reference, not guaranteed to reflect all 2026 perk revisions.
