First hours

  1. After leaving Vault 111, look for the Magnum Opus beginner's guide terminal and its portable holotape.
  2. Practice VATS, aiming and cover before deciding on a weapon specialization.
  3. Set up a modest base for storing supplies. Large settlement projects can wait until you're familiar with Sim Settlements 2.
  4. Follow the Sim Settlements 2 introduction rather than attempting to build every settlement manually.
  5. Explore deliberately. The pack adds activities and encounters beyond vanilla Fallout 4.

Suggested route, not a mandatory quest order.

Combat & armor

Power armor protection

The Bastion system treats power armor as location-based protection. Damaged or broken pieces expose the corresponding body parts, so keep an eye on armor condition.

Armor penetration

Weapon and ammunition choices matter against heavily protected targets. Try higher-penetration options when standard rounds underperform.

Feral ghouls

Ghouls resist damage outside their heads. Headshots are a particularly effective tactic.

These mechanics are described in the author's gameplay FAQ. Exact damage values are not documented here.

Sim Settlements 2

Magnum Opus includes the three-chapter Sim Settlements 2 storyline. Its plot system allows settlers to develop residential, commercial and industrial buildings, with city plans offering broader automation.

Practical starting advice

  • Learn the SS2 introduction before enabling widespread city automation.
  • Keep a manageable settlement as your initial supply hub.
  • Increase settlement build limits cautiously. Higher limits do not eliminate engine performance constraints.
  • Use the SS2-specific quest and system documentation for chapter progression.
