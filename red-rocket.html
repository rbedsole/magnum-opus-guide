Red Rocket → Concord

For your next stretch of gameplay with Dogmeat. A quick reference, not a mandatory route.

Evidence standard: Base-game locations and mechanics are distinguished from installed-mod references and practical recommendations. The chapter does not claim every Magnum Opus interaction has been verified.

Five-minute departure check

  1. Make a manual save before entering unfamiliar areas.
  2. Bring a reliable close-range weapon and a medium-range option, with ammunition for each.
  3. Use Red Rocket as a temporary storage and equipment stop. You do not need a permanent settlement yet.
  4. Watch for adhesive, screws, gears, springs, circuitry and aluminum for crafting.
  5. Check healing supplies and armor condition before heading out.

These are conservative play recommendations. Crafting needs and healing behavior may differ from vanilla Fallout 4.

Where to go next

Concord is the natural early-game direction from Red Rocket in the base game. Approach slowly, use cover and sightlines, and avoid charging into groups. Investigate the Museum of Freedom when ready. Exact encounter changes in Magnum Opus have not been verified.

If your inventory fills, return to a workshop and unload. You do not need to clear every building before continuing.

Combat: what to test

Installed research identifies references to Better Locational Damage and SCOURGE. This does not verify their exact live multipliers or enemy health values.

  • Aim deliberately, use cover and avoid fighting several enemies in the open.
  • Observe how quickly enemies lose health and how dangerous incoming fire feels.
  • Keep a fallback weapon for close encounters.
  • Do not assume specific headshot multipliers, scaling values or perk interactions without confirmation.

Loot without inventory fatigue

Take: useful ammunition, healing supplies, meaningful upgrades and crafting materials you need. Consider: weapons with useful mods or ammunition types. Leave: repeated heavy low-value gear when it forces extra trips.

This is practical advice, not a verified Magnum Opus loot-table or price guide.

Settlements and Sim Settlements 2

Research identifies Sim Settlements 2 and Workshop Framework in the installation. Static configuration references do not prove which settings are active in game.

For now, use Red Rocket as a modest staging area. Learn settlement automation when its gameplay becomes relevant rather than spending your first session designing an economy.

Perks and progression

  • Read your actual in-game perk descriptions before spending points.
  • Carry a little extra healing and ammunition until you know how encounters behave.
  • Don't commit all early resources to construction before understanding your equipment needs.
  • If something behaves unexpectedly, note the exact quest, enemy or item name for later verification.

What's verified and what's not

Base-game knowledge: Red Rocket, Concord, the Museum of Freedom and general crafting resources. Installed-list evidence: references to Better Locational Damage, SCOURGE, SS2 and Workshop Framework. Not yet verified: active mod-specific damage numbers, perk overrides, loot changes and all settlement settings.

The research README reports that the official Magnum Opus FAQ specifies a downgraded Fallout 4 runtime of 1.10.163. Do not assume the Steam installation's Anniversary Edition version is the runtime used by the modlist; no installation changes are recommended here.

Use this guide while playing. Return for help when a real question comes up, not at every checkpoint.
