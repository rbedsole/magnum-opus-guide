Ballistics & Weapon Mechanics

Gameplay guidance with primary sources and clear version caveats.

Commonwealth Ballistics: cover is not invulnerable

The author of Commonwealth Ballistics - Penetration System - F4SE documents engine-level projectile penetration through walls, cover and objects. Penetration depends on ammunition, weapon type, material and thickness. After penetrating an object, bullets lose damage, range and penetration strength.

How to use it in combat

  • Thin cover: Don't assume a wooden barrier will block incoming shots. Move to thicker protection.
  • Heavy cover: Concrete and thicker objects can stop rounds that pass through weaker materials.
  • Different ammunition: Some rounds penetrate more effectively than others. Avoid assuming all guns in the same category behave identically.
  • NPC behavior: The mod's documentation says NPCs can respond to weak cover with suppressive fire and movement.
  • Armor: Armor can further reduce or stop a projectile after it penetrates cover.

How this differs from Bastion

Commonwealth Ballistics models projectiles passing through world geometry and armor. Bastion models power armor pieces protecting individual body parts. Both are included in Magnum Opus, but they are distinct systems. Exact combined damage calculations have not been verified.

Version and compatibility

Magnum Opus's 9.4.0 changelog added Commonwealth Ballistics and its 9.5.0 changelog updated it. The mod's own documentation supports Fallout 4 1.10.163, matching Magnum Opus's stock-game runtime. The exact bundled plugin build still needs verification in MO2.
