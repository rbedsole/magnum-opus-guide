Exploration & Companions

More than the vanilla Commonwealth

Magnum Opus is a content-heavy list. The 9.4.x–9.5.x changelog records additional encounters, interiors, creatures and weapons. Examples include TG'z East Boston Interiors, TG'z North End Interiors, Infested Ferals and Reinforcements and Retribution.

Practical approach: revisit familiar neighborhoods, investigate unmarked interiors, and leave room in your playthrough for side quests rather than rushing the main storyline.

Companion combinations

Heather Casdin does not occupy the standard follower slot, so she can accompany a regular companion. The author also confirms Dogmeat can accompany another companion.

Quality-of-life features

The 9.4.0 changelog notes Show Dialogue Affinity and Map Marker Added Shows Name. The 9.5.0 changelog adds Visible Favorites and Unique Holotapes.

Where to look for exact mod information

Consult the published modlist to identify the responsible mod, then open its individual description for detailed mechanics.
