Fighting smarter in Magnum Opus

Combat & Equipment

Power armor: Bastion

Bastion protects each covered body part until that armor piece is destroyed. This applies to both you and enemies. If a target appears impervious, damage the corresponding armor component or switch to an armor-penetrating weapon.

The author's examples of calibers with penetration include .50 caliber and 2mm EC. Don't assume every gun penetrates armor equally.

Feral ghouls

Feral ghouls have substantial resistance outside the head. Prioritize headshots or VATS targeting instead of wasting ammunition on limbs.

Power armor repairs

Craft Power Armor Repair Kits at the Engineering Workbench, or buy them from appropriate vendors such as the Atom Cats and Brotherhood of Steel.

Recent combat additions

The 9.5.x changelog lists Adaptive Combat Tactics, Adaptive NPC Aim, Commonwealth Ballistics, DirectHit and Med-X Matters among the included or updated mods. These names identify systems in the pack, but their detailed formulas should be checked against the relevant mod pages before making build-specific claims.

Bastion: what the mod author documents

According to Bastion's own documentation, intact power armor pieces can absorb incoming damage entirely. Damage reaches the wearer when a piece breaks or a projectile penetrates it. The system applies to enemies as well as the player.

Bastion's September 2026 changelog also documents that light bladed weapons cannot damage intact power armor, while selected heavy melee weapons, Power Fists and Deathclaw Gauntlets remain effective. These details depend on the installed Bastion version; Magnum Opus's public FAQ confirms the core armor behavior, but not the exact Bastion file version.

Version check: The current Nexus page shows Bastion 2.1.0 (September 2026). Do not assume that is the exact file installed by Magnum Opus without checking its MO2 entry.
